## What I got wrong today, so you don't repeat it

**I told the user granting Screen Recording to Electron "wouldn't reliably
help".** Reasoning: the responsible process is the launching shell, not
`Electron.app`. That is true when *Playwright* drives it — which is why the
automated attempt was denied and no entry ever appeared in System Settings to
grant — and **false** for a normal launch. `npm run app:start`, click Record, and
macOS attributes the request to Electron and prompts. The user tried it anyway
and it worked. Launch method decides the TCC identity.

**I reported a sync number of −1233 ms and nearly published it.** Edge-pairing
found 5 luminance steps in the display and 1 in the camera, paired them by index,
and produced a camera seeing a flash *before* the screen showed it. Not a
measurement — a bug with a decimal point. Auto-exposure ramps rather than steps.
Cross-correlation over the whole signal gives 65 ms.

**Two of my guards were vacuous and only mutation caught them.** A PiP test that
passed with the compositor wiring removed (the display frame fills that corner
either way), and a floor assertion that passed with a term deleted (the bound's
own slack absorbed it). Both fixed with positive discriminators. The peer made
the identical mistake the same day, independently.

**I broke `gate:export` by deleting a helper inside a block I replaced**, and the
gate told me instantly. Run the gate after editing the gate.

## Traps added to CLAUDE.md today

Each cost real time:

- every gate must tear down through `closeQuietly` — #30 missed `seek-gate.mjs`,
  which then held a CI job for 17.5 minutes after failing correctly in 10 seconds
- the job's worst case is a SUM of per-gate process bounds, not a model of their
  internals; a model rots silently and had already rotted twice
- a bound's own slack hides a missing term in its floor — assert COMPOSITION
- ask the ERROR whether a bound fired; do not match its text
- `gate-bounds.mjs` owns every bound and `gate-retry.mjs` imports them; the
  reverse is a cycle that ESM resolves by hanging on the top-level await
- capture must pick a camera by `transportType`, not `devices.first` — `'virt'`
  is a virtual device that idles at ~1 fps
- the camera-to-display sync method, and why `scratch/avsync.cjs` is not it
- Screen Recording TCC for the dev app depends on how it was launched

## One thing that keeps happening

**A default fixed in one place while another path keeps its own copy.** Three
times now:

1. trim — the CLI gate hardcoded a project, so once trim existed the two sides
   exported different things and `test:slow` reported it as a hash mismatch
   between two correct implementations
2. `export-one.mjs` — same defect surviving in the one script whose entire job is
   producing the artifact a human verifies; it exported a camera take with the
   PiP disabled and the reviewer correctly reported seeing no PiP
3. today — `parseProject` learned to default the PiP on for a camera take, while
   `export-one`, `export-gate` and `sink-identity` kept hand-rolling projects
   without one. The app previewed a PiP; the CLI exported none

Fixed structurally this time: callers pass the take's `project.json` **through**
(or `null`) and `harness/export.ts` calls `parseProject`. Assembling a project
outside the one parser is how two answers happen. If you add a fourth caller,
pass the raw document.
